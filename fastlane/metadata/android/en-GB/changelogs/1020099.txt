### Added
- Reminders can count from a task's start as well as its due date.
- All-day tasks can have reminders at set times, e.g. 09:00 and 17:00.
- Other apps can read and edit your local tasks through an OpenTasks provider, with your permission.

### Changed
- Reminders sync as VALARMs, so they also ring in Apple Reminders, Thunderbird and tasks.org.

### Fixed
- Widgets work again after ticking off tasks.
- Signing in by email works again for mailbox.org and Posteo.
