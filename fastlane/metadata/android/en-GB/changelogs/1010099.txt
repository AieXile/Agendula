### Added
- Tags: add them to tasks, browse them from the home screen and filter any list by them. They sync as CATEGORIES.
- Filters: save your own view of open tasks by due date, list, tag and priority, and show it in a widget.
- New widgets: Today, Up next, Lists and Week. A widget can show several lists.

### Changed
- Agenda-style rows in the Tasks widget.
- Synced lists can be renamed and deleted.

### Fixed
- All-day tasks no longer land a day off west of UTC.
